THE WHY

We’re not here to rush you.
We’re here to help you begin.

Waiting Kills is a specialty coffee concept for people with ideas in motion. The founders, the freelancers, the late starters, the early risers — anyone who knows that momentum is made from small, deliberate choices.

We take coffee seriously, but never ourselves. We care about where our beans come from, how each cup is made, and how a space can make you feel: welcome, focused, and ready for what’s next.
